Connection

Native messaging host · com.rapidfetch.host

The desktop app registers the native host automatically when it runs. If the connection fails, open RapidFetch once from the Start menu.

Capturing downloads

Tips

  • Right-click a link → Download with RapidFetch.
  • Right-click a page → Download all links opens a picker with filters.
  • On pages with video or audio, click the blue Download this video button.
  • Alt + Shift + R toggles automatic capture.
  • HLS playlists are detected even when the player hides the real segment URLs.

About

Extension 1.0.0

RapidFetch works entirely on this computer. The extension only talks to 127.0.0.1 and to the native host; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
