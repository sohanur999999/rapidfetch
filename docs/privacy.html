Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

RapidFetch does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Downloads, history, and settings stay on your computer. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking scripts.

RapidFetch is a Windows download manager with an optional browser extension for Chromium browsers. This policy explains what the desktop app and the extension do with your information.

1. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or account credentials
  • Your browsing history
  • Your download history
  • The files you download
  • Your IP address for analytics or advertising
  • Device fingerprints, telemetry, or crash reports

RapidFetch has no advertising network, no analytics service, and no user account system.

2. Information processed on your device

Browser extension

The extension reads the current page to find downloadable media and links. It may process the page URL and title, media and download URLs, file names, sizes, content types, download status, and your settings. This information is used only to show download options and send the selected download to the RapidFetch desktop app on the same computer.

Settings are stored in chrome.storage and may sync through your browser account if Chrome sync is enabled. Tab-specific media state is kept in session storage and is cleared when the tab closes or navigates.

Desktop app

The app stores settings, download history, and queue state in %APPDATA%\RapidFetch. Downloaded files are saved to the folders you choose. Logs in the same folder may contain download URLs and error messages. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Native messaging and localhost bridge

The extension talks to the desktop app through Chrome native messaging (com.rapidfetch.host) or a local bridge on 127.0.0.1. This connection stays on your computer.

3. Network connections

RapidFetch connects only to:

  • the download addresses you choose;
  • the local RapidFetch desktop app;
  • official download locations for yt-dlp, ffmpeg, or similar tools when you ask the app to install or update them;
  • video sites you choose to resolve through yt-dlp.

Those sites and tools have their own privacy policies. RapidFetch does not proxy or monitor your traffic.

If you enable Cookies from browser for the optional yt-dlp integration, RapidFetch passes cookies from your chosen browser profile to the local yt-dlp process so it can access a video you are allowed to download. The cookies are not sent to us.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data. We have no server that receives your downloads, browsing history, or extension activity. The only third-party processing is performed by services you already use, such as Chrome sync for extension settings, or by the websites and tools you choose to access.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Extension: remove it in chrome://extensions to delete its local and synced settings. You can also clear its data from the extension details page.
  • Desktop app: remove download records in the app, or delete %APPDATA%\RapidFetch after closing the app. Uninstalling keeps this folder so your settings and history are not lost; delete it manually for a full reset.
  • Downloaded files: deleting a history record does not delete the file unless you choose Also delete files from storage in the remove dialog.

6. Permissions used by the extension

PermissionWhy it is needed
downloadsCapture browser downloads and provide pause, resume, cancel, and reveal actions.
storageSave settings and tab-specific media state.
contextMenusAdd the Download with RapidFetch right-click menu.
webRequestDetect media responses, content types, and sizes while you browse.
tabsRead the active tab and keep media state per tab.
notificationsShow download progress and completion actions.
scriptingRead media and link URLs from the current page when you request it.
nativeMessagingCommunicate with the RapidFetch desktop app.
<all_urls>Detect downloadable media on any site, because media can be served from any domain.

The extension does not modify page content, inject ads, or change your search engine or new-tab page.

7. Children

RapidFetch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the repository and the effective date will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the release notes.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, open an issue at github.com/sohanur999999/rapidfetch/issues.

10. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

RapidFetch's use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide or improve the extension's single purpose: capturing downloads and media and handing them to the RapidFetch desktop app on the same computer.
