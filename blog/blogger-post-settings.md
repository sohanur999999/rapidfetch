## Publishing steps

1. Open [Blogger](https://www.blogger.com/) and select the **BroBuddy** blog.
2. Click **New post**.
3. Paste the title:
   `RapidFetch: Free IDM Alternative for Windows and Chrome`
4. Switch the editor from **Compose** to **HTML view**.
5. Open `blog/rapidfetch-free-idm-alternative-blogger.html`, copy all of it, and
   paste it into the HTML editor.
6. In **Post settings**, set the custom permalink to
   `rapidfetch-free-idm-alternative`.
7. Add the **Search Description** exactly as written above.
8. Add the five labels.
9. Preview the post on desktop and mobile. Check the table, download buttons,
   and image widths.
10. Click **Publish**.
11. Open the published URL, check it returns 200, and submit the URL in Google
    Search Console if the site is connected there.
12. Add the new post to the **Tech** navigation or feature it from a related
    post if you want faster internal discovery.

## SEO notes

- The Blogger post title is the page H1. The body deliberately starts with an
  intro and uses H2/H3 headings, not another H1.
- Primary keyword: **free IDM alternative**.
- Secondary phrases used naturally: **IDM alternative for Windows**, **free
  download manager**, **Chrome download manager**, **video downloader for
  Chrome**, **HLS downloader**, **resumable downloads**.
- The first screen includes the primary phrase, a one-sentence answer, and a
  clear limitation. That improves trust and reduces bounce from users who need
  one-click Chrome Web Store installation.
- The comparison table is factual and avoids unsupported speed claims.
- The post links to the official product page, GitHub source, release, privacy
  policy, and the BroBuddy Tech Help label. It does not link to cracked software.
- The hero image filename and alt text contain the target phrase without keyword
  stuffing.
- Keep the “Last updated” date current when you edit the post. Freshness matters
  for software guides.

## Do not change without checking

- Do not add a Chrome Web Store button. The listing is not live yet.
- Do not describe RapidFetch as faster than IDM. No side-by-side benchmark was
  run.
- Do not claim macOS, Linux, Android, or iOS support.
- Do not claim DRM bypassing or paid-streaming extraction.
- Keep the IDM/Tonec disclaimer and the “download only content you have
  permission to save” language.
